# WET-104 · memes

`^ REFINE EACH Post %Meme` — each incept post recut as an image macro.
The words do not change. The line splits into a top line and a bottom line,
set in Anton, white on black bars, over a 4:5 crop of the same still.
Australian spelling kept (`APOLOGISES`).

The picture is the post. A scroller gets the sentence as a meme and the
frame with no explanation, which is the same planted thought as the
incept post: *what the hell is going on?*

- Reel 1 crops the reel-1 still (1.2 s) so the spool stays whole: the glove,
  the red thread running off the table, the wooden core, and the rim of a
  petri dish. The hand is the subject; nothing labels it.
- Reel 2 crops the reel-2 still (2.8 s) on the aisle: the neon heartbeat,
  the bags on the meat rail, the drain gutter running to the bottom edge.
  An empty room with a punchline.
